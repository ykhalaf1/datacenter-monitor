How the Environmental Impact Score Works
Each facility is scored 0–100 (lower = greener) across four factors: Renewable Energy % (30%) · Power Usage Effectiveness (25%) · Water Usage (25%) · Capacity (MW) (20%). Click the Environmental Impact column header to sort by score.
